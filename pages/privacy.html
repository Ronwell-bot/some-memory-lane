Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Camera Access

Some Memory Lane requests camera access when you choose to start a photo session. Camera access is controlled by your browser and requires your permission before the camera can be used. The camera is stopped when the session is completed.

Photo Storage

Photos are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers unless future cloud-saving features are added and enabled by you.

Personal Information

Some Memory Lane does not require personal information to use the basic site.

Cookies & Local Storage

Basic browser storage may be used to remember preferences such as themes, music, or layout choices.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the project grows. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
